How It Works

Three steps to member glow.

1 · Tell us about you
Fill out the form with your details, pick your plan, and agree to the membership terms.
2 · Set up your monthly payment
Right after you submit, we'll send you to our secure Square checkout to start your plan. Your card stays safely with Square and bills on the 1st of each month.
3 · Sign your agreement
Within a business day, your Membership Agreement arrives by email for a quick electronic signature — then you're official. Time to book that first facial.
The fine print, in plain English
  • Your membership is a one-year commitment, then continues month-to-month (cancel anytime after the first year, no penalty).
  • Ending early has a one-time fee of six months of membership dues.
  • Covers bar facials and chemical peels only — treatment-room services get member benefits applied, you pay the difference.
  • Facials don't roll over month to month, but you can gift an unused facial to a friend or relative.
  • Facials can't be converted to gift cards or used toward products or gratuity. Gratuity isn't included in your monthly dues.
  • Billing runs on the 1st of each month for that month. If a payment fails and we can't reach you within 3 days, the membership is cancelled.
  • Perks: 10% off products and boosters year-round, and the private-event fee is waived for events hosted in the facial bar.
  • Valid at both Birmingham and Pelham. Hours, menu, and pricing may change — we'll keep you posted.
