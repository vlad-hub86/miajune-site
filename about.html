Meet the Founder

Magen Cash

Founder & Licensed Master Esthetician

As a Licensed Master Aesthetician, Magen has been passionately dedicated to the skincare industry for over a decade. After earning her aesthetics license in Richmond, Virginia, she returned to her hometown of Birmingham, Alabama, where she gained invaluable experience working alongside dermatologists and plastic surgeons.

While she loved her time in the clinical setting, Magen envisioned a different kind of skincare experience — more relaxed, fun, and accessible. In August 2018, she brought that vision to life with Fancy Face, and in January 2025 began a new chapter: Mia June Facial Bar — a name that carries the same love, passion, and commitment that has always defined the brand.
