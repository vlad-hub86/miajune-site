Introducing the Iced Revival Facial ❄️

By Allison Kravchuk

Summer can leave skin feeling hot, tired, puffy, and dull. That's why we've created the perfect seasonal reset.

Introducing the Iced Revival Facial — a cooling, glow-boosting treatment designed to refresh your skin and revive your complexion.

This limited-time facial features chilled stainless steel ice globes that gently glide across the skin to help reduce puffiness, calm inflammation, and leave you feeling instantly refreshed. Paired with Éminence's luxurious Raspberry Pore Refining Mask, this treatment helps smooth, brighten, and refine the appearance of pores for a healthy, radiant glow.

Think of it as an icy refresh for your skin on a hot summer day.

The Iced Revival Facial leaves skin feeling cool, soothed, smooth, and beautifully refreshed. It's the perfect treatment for anyone looking to revive tired summer skin, enhance their glow, and enjoy a little extra self-care during the warmer months.
