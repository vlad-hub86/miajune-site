Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your first glow. Still have a question? Email info@miajunefacialbar.com.

What should I expect at my first facial?

Your esthetician begins with a short consultation about your skin goals and any sensitivities, then customizes your treatment. A typical facial includes cleansing, exfoliation, extractions as needed, massage, and a tailored Éminence Organic mask. Plan to arrive a few minutes early to complete a brief intake form.

How often should I get a facial?

For most skin types we recommend a facial every 4 to 6 weeks, which follows the skin's natural renewal cycle. Our monthly memberships are designed around this cadence.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is handled online through Square Appointments. Choose your location on our Book page and reserve your time. You may be asked to sign in with Square and add a card on file before booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. Cancellations with less than one day's notice may be charged a $50 fee, and a no-show without cancellation will be charged the full price of the booked services.

How should I prepare for my facial?

Please discontinue all topical treatments and retinoids for a minimum of 3 days before and after your facial. Come with clean skin if possible, and let your esthetician know about any recent sun exposure, medications, or skin treatments.

What skincare products do you use?

Every treatment at Mia June uses Éminence Organic Skin Care — biodynamic, farm-fresh ingredients formulated by herbalists. It's certified organic, responsibly sourced, and results-driven.

Do you offer memberships and gift cards?

Yes. We offer monthly memberships with one or two facials per month, plus gift cards for all locations. See options on our Book page.

Where are you located?

We have three locations: Birmingham, AL (2205 7th Ave S), Pelham, AL (2152-A Pelham Pkwy), and Oxford, MS (653 Concordia Ave). Find hours and directions on our Book page.
