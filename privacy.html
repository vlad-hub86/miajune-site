Privacy Policy.

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Mia June Facial Bar ("Mia June," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or one of our locations in Birmingham, AL; Pelham, AL; or Oxford, MS, and how we use and protect it.

Information We Collect

  • Booking & contact information — your name, email address, phone number, and appointment details when you book a service, purchase a membership or gift card, or contact us.
  • Payment information — payments and card-on-file details are processed and stored securely by Square, our booking and payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own systems.
  • Skin & health intake — information you share on intake forms so our estheticians can tailor and safely perform your treatment.
  • Usage information — basic analytics such as pages visited and device type, collected to help us improve the site.

How We Use Your Information

  • To schedule, confirm, and manage your appointments and memberships.
  • To process payments and apply our cancellation policy.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer service.
  • To send appointment reminders and, where you have opted in, occasional news and promotions. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our website and services.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers who help us operate — such as Square (booking and payments) and our website and analytics providers — and only as needed to deliver our services, or when required by law.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Third-party tools such as Square and web analytics may set their own cookies subject to their own privacy policies.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and retain it only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may opt out of marketing messages at any time, by contacting us using the details below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Guests under 18 should have parental or guardian consent for treatment.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and we will revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at info@miajunefacialbar.com (Oxford: oxford.ms@miajunefacialbar.com).

This policy is provided for general information and is not legal advice. We recommend having it reviewed by qualified counsel to ensure it meets the requirements that apply to your business.
